01 — Our Story

Why We Love Cats

Nine millennia of companionship, distilled into moments of quiet wonder.

Cats have lived alongside humans for over 9,000 years. Independent yet deeply affectionate, they bring warmth to homes across every continent.

Whether curled in a sunbeam or mid-pounce, cats remind us to slow down, observe, and find beauty in the smallest moments.

03 — Did You Know

Remarkable Facts

Behind those whiskers lies a world of fascinating biology and ancient history.

😴

Champions of Sleep

Cats sleep 12–16 hours a day, conserving energy for their next great adventure — or nap.

👂

Super Hearing

20+ muscles per ear, rotating 180° to pinpoint the faintest rustle of a treat bag.

💫

Healing Purr

Purrs vibrate at 25–150 Hz — frequencies scientifically linked to bone healing.

🏛️

Sacred in Egypt

Ancient Egyptians worshipped Bastet. Harming a cat was punishable by death.

🐾

A Clowder

A group of cats is a clowder. Three kittens? That's a kindle.

🎯

Night Vision

Cats need one-sixth the light humans do. Their tapetum makes eyes glow at night.

04 — The Collection

Iconic Breeds

From regal Persians to chatty Siamese — each breed carries its own legend.

Royal

Persian

Luxurious coat, calm temperament. The undisputed royalty of the cat world.

Vocal

Siamese

Striking blue eyes and a voice that won't be ignored. Social and opinionated.

Giant

Maine Coon

One of the largest breeds. Friendly, fluffy, built for harsh winters.

Classic

British Shorthair

Round face, plush coat, easygoing nature. The quintessential teddy bear.
