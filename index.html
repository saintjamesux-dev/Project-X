🐱 All About Cats

Curious, graceful, and endlessly fascinating companions

Why We Love Cats

Cats have lived alongside humans for thousands of years. Independent yet affectionate, they bring warmth and quiet joy to homes around the world. Whether they're curled up in a sunbeam or chasing a toy, cats remind us to slow down and enjoy the moment.

Fun Facts

  • Cats sleep 12–16 hours a day on average.
  • A group of cats is called a clowder.
  • Cats have over 20 muscles controlling each ear.
  • They can rotate their ears 180 degrees to pinpoint sounds.
  • A cat's purr may help heal bones and reduce stress.

Popular Breeds

  • Persian — Long, luxurious coat and a calm temperament.
  • Siamese — Vocal, social, and striking blue eyes.
  • Maine Coon — One of the largest breeds, friendly and fluffy.
  • British Shorthair — Round face, plush coat, easygoing nature.
Did you know? Ancient Egyptians revered cats and even worshipped a cat goddess named Bastet.
