Why We Love Cats

Did You Know

Remarkable Facts

Behind those whiskers lies a world of fascinating biology and behavior.

😴

Champions of Sleep

Cats sleep 12–16 hours a day. They're conserving energy for their next great adventure — or nap.

👂

Super Hearing

Over 20 muscles control each ear, rotating 180° to pinpoint the faintest rustle of a treat bag.

💫

Healing Purr

A cat's purr vibrates at 25–150 Hz — frequencies linked to bone healing and stress reduction.

🏛️

Sacred in Egypt

Ancient Egyptians worshipped Bastet, the cat goddess. Harming a cat was punishable by death.

🐾

A Clowder

A group of cats is called a clowder. Three or more kittens? That's a kindle.

🎯

Night Vision

Cats need only one-sixth the light humans do to see. Their eyes glow because of a reflective layer called the tapetum.

Iconic Breeds

From regal Persians to chatty Siamese — each breed brings its own personality.

Persian

Long, luxurious coat. Calm, gentle temperament. The royalty of the cat world.

Siamese

Vocal, social, and striking blue eyes. They'll tell you exactly what they think.

Maine Coon

One of the largest breeds. Friendly, fluffy, and built for cold winters.

British Shorthair

Round face, plush coat, easygoing nature. The quintessential teddy bear cat.
